The question
2.1.1 is about how the website specifically should look and feel — its design language, UX priorities, content hierarchy.

2.6 is about the overall visual direction across everything — brand photography style, color application, typography expression, design system that informs the website, social, ads, and all other channels.

They're different scopes but the same name. Options below.
A

Rename 2.6 → "Visual Direction"

Clean separation. "Creative Direction" stays website-specific. "Visual Direction" covers the brand-wide visual tone.

B

Rename 2.6 → "Brand Expression"

Broader than just visual — covers how the brand shows up across all touchpoints. Photography, design system, visual tone, typography application.

C

Rename 2.1.1 → "Web Design Direction"

Keep 2.6 as "Creative Direction" (it's the bigger concept). Make the website subsection more specific instead.

D

Merge them — no separate 2.6

The overall creative direction lives in Brand Strategy (1.0) or gets absorbed into each channel section. No standalone 2.6 needed.
