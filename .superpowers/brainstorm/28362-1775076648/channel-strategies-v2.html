Channel Strategies — Updated

Revised based on your feedback. S3 Media Strategy is now correctly defined as the photo/video shoot direction that gets turned over to the S3 Media team — not earned/owned/paid media mix.

1

Website Strategy

Site architecture direction, UX priorities, conversion path strategy, content hierarchy.

2

SEO Strategy

Keyword targeting, content strategy for organic, technical SEO priorities, local vs national approach.

3

Paid Advertising Strategy

Platform selection, budget allocation, audience targeting, conversion goals, campaign structure.

4

Social Media Strategy

Platform prioritization, content pillars, posting cadence direction, community approach.

5

S3 Media Strategy

Photo shoot and/or video shoot creative direction. This section becomes the turnover to the S3 Media team — what to capture, the visual tone, shot list direction, talent/location notes. Paired with a mood board when handed off. This is why the creative brief exists as a standalone document separate from the creative turnover.

6

Creative Direction

Visual tone, design language, brand expression guidelines. Distinct from S3 Media — this is about the overall look and feel across all touchpoints, not the shoot specifically.

7

Content Strategy

Content pillars, editorial voice, content types and formats, blog/resource strategy. Cross-cuts multiple channels.

8

The Bright Idea

The creative throughline. Could be one master idea that threads everything, or channel-specific ideas. Lives here in the strategy, gets translated into executable direction in the creative briefs.
