Channel Strategies — What's in scope?

These are the strategic domains that could appear in a Strategy Brief. Not all will be used for every project — only the ones relevant to the engagement. Select any that should be removed or let me know what's missing.

1

Website Strategy

Site architecture direction, UX priorities, conversion path strategy, content hierarchy. Feeds into s3-creative-brief-website.

2

SEO Strategy

Keyword targeting, content strategy for organic, technical SEO priorities, local vs national approach. Informed by SEO retainer line item and foundational brief's digital snapshot.

3

Paid Advertising Strategy

Platform selection (Google Ads, Meta, programmatic), budget allocation, audience targeting, conversion goals, campaign structure. Feeds into s3-creative-brief-paid-ads.

4

Social Media Strategy

Platform prioritization, content pillars, posting cadence direction, community approach, influencer/partnership considerations. Feeds into s3-creative-brief-social-media.

5

Media Strategy

Earned/owned/paid media mix, PR direction, media placements, thought leadership distribution. Feeds into s3-creative-brief-media.

6

Creative Direction

Visual tone, photography/video direction, design language, brand expression guidelines. Informs the creative turnover and wireframes.

7

Content Strategy

Content pillars, editorial voice, content types and formats, blog/resource strategy, content calendar direction. Cross-cuts multiple channels.

8

The Bright Idea

The creative throughline — could be one master idea that threads everything, or channel-specific ideas that serve different purposes. This is where the strategic concept lives before it gets translated into executable direction in the creative briefs.

Click any that DON'T belong, or tell me in the terminal what's missing.
