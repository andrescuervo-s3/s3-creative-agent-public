# Competitor Profile Research Protocol (Studio 3 Marketing)

Purpose: Generate up to 6 credible, evidence-based competitor profiles for a client using third-party research only. Each profile must include: Name, Overview (2-4 sentences), URL (most relevant page), and Proof Signals (2-5 specific, verifiable indicators of credibility).

---

## Source Eligibility Rules

### Approved Source Types
- Directory listings or review platforms:
  - Legal: Avvo, Super Lawyers, Martindale-Hubbell
  - Healthcare: Healthgrades, RealSelf, Vitals
  - Home Services: Angi, Houzz, Yelp, BBB
  - B2B/Professional Services: G2, Capterra, Clutch
- Firm websites: only to confirm services, specialties, leadership, or awards
- Google organic listings: to establish local prominence or visibility
- News or media mentions: only from reputable, independent outlets

### Do Not Use
- Paid ads or sponsored placements
- Marketing agency blogs or vendor-published lists
- AI-generated lists or unverified directories
- Self-promotional claims unless validated externally

---

## Research Instructions

### Step 1: Identify Priority Service Area and Region
Use client documents, intake notes, or SEO terms to identify:
- Geographic service area (e.g., Orlando, Southern California, NYC metro)
- Core service category (e.g., divorce, injectables, SaaS, HVAC)
- Sector type (use industry category examples below for reference)

### Step 2: Perform Search for Top Organic Providers
- Search for "[primary service] + [location]" in Google
  - Example: "DUI attorney Orlando" or "facial rejuvenation Tampa"
- Click only on organic (non-ad) results that appear on page 1-2
- Select firms that:
  - Appear consistently across multiple directories
  - Have a dedicated service focus in the relevant category
  - Offer clear, public-facing positioning

### Step 3: Validate Against Third-Party Directories
For each identified competitor:
- Check if the provider has a presence on one or more of the following (based on vertical):
  - Law: Avvo, Super Lawyers, Martindale-Hubbell
  - Aesthetics: Healthgrades, RealSelf, Vitals
  - Home Services: Yelp, BBB, Angi
  - Tech/Professional Services: G2, Clutch, Capterra

Capture:
- Credentials or years of experience
- Verified reviews (volume, average rating, recency)
- Awards, badges, or certifications
- Public case studies, outcomes, or media mentions
- Notable clients, partnerships, or affiliations

### Step 4: Write Competitor Profile (Structured Format)
For each of up to 6 competitors, include:

**Name:** Official brand or firm name

**Overview:** 2-4 sentences summarizing:
- Primary service category
- Geographic focus
- Unique emphasis in service delivery (e.g., aggressive litigation, holistic skincare, complex systems integrations)
- Stated value proposition or client promise

**URL:** Firm's homepage or most relevant practice/service page (non-ad URL)

**Proof Signals:** List 2-5 concrete credibility signals with specific language, such as:
- "Rated 4.8 stars from 215 reviews on Avvo"
- "Listed in Super Lawyers 2024 -- Family Law"
- "BBB A+ rating with 40+ verified reviews"
- "Google Business profile with 4.7 stars and 90 reviews"
- "Over 10 years serving the Orlando area"

## Final Deliverable Requirements

Each profile must be:
- Evidence-based
- Free of editorializing
- Focused on how the competitor presents themselves
- Cited with proof signals from public sources
- Structurally consistent with other entries
